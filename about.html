I. About

Max Bissonnette is a New York City-based student, composer, writer, and artist interested in exploring the intersection between the arts, politics, and advocacy.

Outside of composing, Max has worked as a constituent services intern in the New York State Assembly, is the Deputy Director of Finance at We the Students, a 501(c)(4) organization dedicated to civic education and advocacy, and has experience working on national political campaigns. They have served in student government, co-led Model UN, and edited their school's literary magazine and newspaper.

Their work has been recognized by the Scholastic Art and Writing Awards, The Tony Awards, Luna Composition Lab, The Juilliard School, The New York Philharmonic, and others.
